About NowYogi

NowYogi brings your yoga class home. Your teacher's sequences, a calm voice guide and a clear demonstration of every pose help you keep practising between classes, at your own pace.

Practise any time

Choose a program, press Start and follow along. No sign-up and no subscription.

Guided by voice

A female voice guide explains each asana, counts your breaths and tells you what comes next, so you can stay on the mat.

Built around you

Your profile flags poses to take care with. Build your own cycles or import one from your teacher.

Practise safely

Yoga should never hurt. Use the easier option, rest in Child's Pose whenever you need, and talk to your doctor about any health condition.
